How to trace

With the camera

  1. Prop your phone 20 to 30 cm above the paper, camera facing down. A phone stand, a glass, or two stacks of books work.
  2. Look at the screen, not the paper. Drag, pinch and twist the picture until it sits where you want it on your page.
  3. Lower the opacity until you can see your pencil through the picture.
  4. Tap Lock so stray touches can't move the picture.
  5. Trace while watching the screen. Tap Hide image now and then to check your drawing.

Without a stand (light pad)

  1. Turn off Camera and turn your screen brightness all the way up.
  2. Set the opacity to 100%, size the picture, and tap Lock.
  3. Lay thin paper on the screen and trace lightly.

Tips: Clear whites makes white areas see-through, which works best with the Outline view. Double-tap the picture to hide or show the toolbar. To unlock, press and hold the lock button in the corner.
